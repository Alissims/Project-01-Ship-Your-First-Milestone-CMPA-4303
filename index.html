Why it exists

Aunt Mae's Treats started the way most good food does — in a home kitchen, with recipes worth sharing past the family table. What began as jars for friends and family turned into a small, hands-on business: preserves cooked in small batches, sold directly, one order at a time.

There's no storefront yet. Just real ingredients, careful batches, and a jar that tastes like it was made by someone who cares whether you like it.
